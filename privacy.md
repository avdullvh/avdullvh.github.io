# Sync — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 October 2026 (site address updated)

Sync is a video collaboration workspace at https://sync.weakenedits.workers.dev/ operated by Abdullah (contact: weakenedits@gmail.com). This policy explains what Sync collects and how it is used.

## Information we collect

**Account information.** Your email address and a password (stored by our authentication provider, Supabase, in hashed form). If you sign in with Google, we receive your Google account email address, name and profile picture URL. We use only your verified email address to identify your account.

**Workspace content.** Projects, video tasks, assignments, due dates, comments, approvals, project invitations and the preview videos you or your collaborators upload.

**Technical data.** A secure session cookie that keeps you signed in, and standard server logs such as IP address and request time.

## How we use it

We use this information only to provide Sync: to sign you in, control who can access each project, deliver previews and comments to project members, and send account emails such as email confirmation and password reset. We do not sell your information and do not use it for advertising.

## Google user data

Data received through Google sign-in is used only to create and sign in to your Sync account. It is not shared with third parties except the service providers listed below, and it is not used to train AI models. Sync's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

## Service providers

Supabase (authentication and video storage), Cloudflare (application hosting and database) and Google (Gmail, for sending account emails). They process data on our behalf to run the service.

## Sharing within Sync

Content in a project is visible to the members of that project according to their role (owner, editor or client). Project owners decide who is invited.

## Retention and deletion

We keep your account and workspace content while your account is active. To delete your account or request a copy of your data, email weakenedits@gmail.com and we will act on the request within 30 days.

## Security

Access to private projects and media is checked on the server for every request. Sessions use secure, HTTP-only cookies. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this address with a new date.
